From Brahmanbaria to BUET

Shova grew up in Brahmanbaria in a family with very little money, and for a while without a permanent home address. She went from a village school to one of the hardest engineering universities in Bangladesh to get into, and national media wrote about it.

Scholarships from the Ghuddi Foundation, the Brac Bank-Prothom Alo Trust and a few local education groups made that possible. Since then she has spent several years volunteering with the same kinds of scholarship programs.
