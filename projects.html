Capstone Project · Team Leader · BUET

Redevelopment of Dhaka's Nilkhet Book Market

Project Overview
Led a multidisciplinary capstone project to redevelop Dhaka's iconic Nilkhet Book Market into a sustainable seven-storey mixed-use commercial complex. The project integrated urban planning, structural, geotechnical, transportation, environmental, and socio-economic analyses while preserving the area's cultural identity.
Role
Served as Team Leader, coordinating project planning, technical analyses, stakeholder discussions, and design integration across multiple civil engineering disciplines throughout the project lifecycle.
Technical Scope
Conducted feasibility, financial, and economic analyses, structural design using an IMRF system with mat foundation, traffic impact assessment, environmental sustainability evaluation, BOQ preparation, and SWOT analysis to assess long-term viability.
Outcome
Successfully defended the project with a comprehensive redevelopment proposal featuring organized commercial spaces, underground parking, improved pedestrian circulation, enhanced fire safety systems, a central atrium, and resilient infrastructure designed to improve accessibility while supporting Nilkhet's role as Bangladesh's leading academic marketplace.

Computation Sessional · MATLAB · Sep 2023

Urban Flood Prediction Tool

Project Overview
Developed a MATLAB-based computational tool to assess urban stormwater runoff and flood risk in Dhanmondi, Dhaka. The project integrates hydrologic and hydraulic modeling to estimate runoff, flood depth, drainage performance, and infrastructure capacity under varying urbanization and rainfall scenarios.
Method
Implemented the Rational Method for runoff estimation, Manning's Equation for pipe flow analysis, and a grid-based numerical simulation to model flood depth. The application features an interactive GUI where users can adjust rainfall intensity, duration, land-use distribution, and pipe diameter to visualize flood behavior and drainage performance.
Key Features
  • Interactive MATLAB GUI for rapid flood assessment
  • 3D flood inundation visualization
  • Spatial flood risk hotspot heat map
  • Peak runoff and flood depth estimation
  • Stagnant water volume calculation
  • Recommended drainage pipe diameter based on simulation results
Outcome
Delivered a functional decision-support tool capable of predicting urban flood risk and evaluating drainage system performance. The project demonstrates how computational modeling can support sustainable urban drainage planning and infrastructure resilience in rapidly urbanizing cities.
