Capstone project at BUET. I was the team leader.

Redevelopment of Dhaka's Nilkhet Book Market

Our team redesigned Nilkhet Book Market as a seven-storey mixed-use commercial complex. The work covered urban planning, structural and geotechnical design, traffic, environmental and socio-economic studies, and we tried to keep the character of the area as Dhaka's book market. As leader I planned the work, kept the technical parts consistent with each other, and handled discussions with stakeholders.

The technical work included feasibility, financial and economic analysis, and the BOQ. The structure uses an IMRF system on a mat foundation, and we added a traffic impact assessment, an environmental review and a SWOT analysis. The final proposal has organized shop spaces, underground parking, better pedestrian movement, improved fire safety and a central atrium. We defended it before a jury board.

Computation Sessional, MATLAB, Sep 2023

Urban Flood Prediction Tool

A MATLAB tool that estimates stormwater runoff and flood risk for Dhanmondi, Dhaka. It uses the Rational Method for runoff, Manning's equation for flow in the pipes, and a grid-based simulation for flood depth. In the GUI you can change rainfall intensity, duration, land use and pipe diameter and see how the flooding responds.

It gives a 3D inundation view, a heat map of risk hotspots, peak runoff and flood depth, the volume of stagnant water, and a suggested drainage pipe diameter. It is meant as a decision-support tool for drainage planning in fast-growing cities.
